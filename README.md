> **I build software where engineering, automation and AI meet.**

I'm a Software Engineer and Founder of **[Pakhreen](https://www.pakhreen.com)**, focused on building practical digital products from concept through to production.

I have a **Master of Information Technology (Software Development)** and hands-on experience across full-stack development, AI-powered applications, REST APIs, cloud platforms, automation and mobile development.
